The problem

The truth is, great rentals don't sell themselves if nobody sees them.

Early 2026 looked like this: no video, about 8 Facebook views a day, Instagram reach near zero, and no clicks to the website. Busy season was coming. The feed was asleep.

The big idea

Show the setups. Show them often. Put a little money behind the one that sells.

  1. Consistent publishing on Facebook and Instagram, starting in March.
  2. A short-video program: 22 videos, most under 20 seconds.
  3. A lean, local ad test on inflatables, tents and lead forms.
  4. A local audience: over half of followers live in ten nearby towns.

What we learned

  1. Short wins. Every top video ran 5 to 21 seconds.
  2. The algorithm is a new-customer machine. 54% of video watch time came from people who did not follow the page yet.
  3. Small budgets work locally. Under $2 a click when the offer is clear and fun.

What's next

Attention is step one. Bookings are the goal. So the next 90 days are about turning eyeballs into inquiries:

  1. Conversion tracking on every quote request and call.
  2. Always-on ads instead of one big burst.
  3. Clear "Get a quote" calls to action on Instagram.
