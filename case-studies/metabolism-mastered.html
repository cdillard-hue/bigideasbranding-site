The challenge

The truth is, Bex was already the real deal. A proven coach. Active on Instagram, Facebook, YouTube and TikTok.

But click through to her website and you got one headline: "Let's do this TOGETHER!" One button. A contact form asking for your name, email, phone and birthday.

So who is Bex? Who does she help? What does she offer? Why trust her? A visitor couldn't tell.

What the old site was missing

  • Who Bex is
  • Who she helps
  • Program descriptions
  • Pricing
  • Testimonials
  • Video
  • A brand voice
  • A real logo, not a tiny screenshot
  • Navigation beyond "Book Online" and "Contact Us"

The audience

Women in midlife, especially those moving through perimenopause and menopause.

They're done with crash diets. Done with endless cardio. Done with generic apps that weren't built for their bodies. They want to rebuild their metabolism in a way that actually lasts.

The brand voice

Warm. Confident. Encouraging. Built around mindset and self-trust, because the body follows the brain.

Every page, caption and video speaks to her like a coach who believes in her, not a drill sergeant yelling at her.

The line that anchors everything

Train Your Body. Retrain Your Brain. Trust Yourself.

This is not a race. It's not a marathon. It's life.
