Our own venture

Hoonah Off Ship

A locally run guide to Hoonah, Alaska. Real places. Real people. Real Alaska.

The challenge

Most cruise passengers only see the port. The real town, and the small local businesses in it, sit a short walk away. Travelers needed one clear, trusted place to find them.

What we built

  • An independent directory with four founding members: bear tours, whale watching, a village tour with crab feast, and a harborfront restaurant
  • A Build your day planner with three sample days
  • A walking and shuttle guide, plus pages for six cruise lines and each activity
  • Passenger questions, answered and written to be found in search
  • An inquiry form for local businesses that want a listing
Visit hoonahoffship.com

The challenge

The customer is a cruise passenger with only a few hours in port. Our client competes with cruise-line excursions, big travel marketplaces, agencies and last-minute sellers. The goal: make the brand recognizable before anyone steps off the ship.

  • Wrote content that answers the questions travelers ask before they arrive, so search engines and AI tools can find and recommend the company

Free guide: hire for great people culture

It takes the right people to turn a big idea into big results. Three steps to get there.

Find the right people

Many people interview well. So how do you weed out the ones who will miss the mark? Look for people who bring equal parts effort, ability and desire. If one is missing, move on to the next candidate.

  • Desire
  • Ability
  • Effort
Clearly define important characteristics

Study your mission and vision statements. Which personality traits matter most? Are your interview questions aligned with them? Ask situational questions to see if a candidate will show the behaviors your brand stands for.

Diagnose root causes

Teach managers to look for root causes, not symptoms. Say the staff keeps forgetting to clean one area. It has happened more than once, so it is a pattern. Stop cleaning up the mistake for them. It can only be one of three things: attitude, training or a broken system.

  • Attitude
  • Training
  • System

Building the people side of your brand can feel confusing. Your people are the engine that drives your business. Let us help you.
